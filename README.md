# Banking Customer Portfolio & Lending Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a banking customer portfolio to understand customer behavior, lending patterns, deposit trends, and business growth opportunities. The objective was to transform raw banking data into actionable business insights using Power BI, Python, DAX, and exploratory data analysis techniques.

The analysis was performed on a dataset containing 3,000 customer records across deposits, loans, savings accounts, checking accounts, business lending, customer demographics, loyalty classifications, and risk indicators.

Rather than simply building visualizations, the goal of this project was to identify meaningful business patterns and generate recommendations that could support strategic decision-making within a banking environment.

---

## Business Problem

Financial institutions generate large volumes of customer and transaction data. However, the true value of this data lies in its ability to support business decisions.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

* Which customer segments contribute the most value to the bank?
* What is driving lending performance?
* Are there concentration risks within the portfolio?
* Which customers should be prioritized for retention?
* What opportunities exist for cross-selling and revenue growth?

## Key Findings

### Business Lending Drives Portfolio Growth

Business lending contributes ₹2.60 billion and represents the largest lending segment within the portfolio. While this strengthens revenue generation, it also increases dependence on commercial borrowers.

### Medium-Income Customers Are the Core Customer Segment

Medium-income customers account for the broadest engagement across deposits, loans, and savings products. This segment forms the backbone of the bank's customer base.

### Hidden Opportunity Within the Jade Loyalty Tier

Customers classified under the Jade loyalty segment exhibit surprisingly high deposit and lending balances. This suggests potential opportunities for customer upgrades and retention programs.

### European Customers Lead Portfolio Volume

European customers contribute the largest share of overall deposits and lending activity, making them one of the bank's most valuable customer groups.

### Healthy Liquidity Position

The loan-to-deposit ratio remains below 1.0, indicating that the bank is maintaining a balanced relationship between lending activities and funding sources.

## Strategic Recommendations

* Strengthen retention programs for medium-income customers.
* Review loyalty-tier assignment criteria and customer upgrade strategies.
* Expand commercial banking relationship management.
* Implement proactive monitoring for higher-risk customer segments.
* Develop wealth-management offerings for high-value customers.
* Increase cross-selling opportunities across existing customer relationships.
